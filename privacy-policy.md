Privacy Policy – Personal Macro Remote Control Bot

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Purpose

This Discord bot is designed for personal use. Its primary purpose is to remotely control a macro running on the owner's computer through Discord commands.

2. Information collected

The bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and commands sent to it to verify authorization and operate its features.

3. Data usage

Information is used exclusively to process commands, verify authorized users, and communicate with the macro.

4. Data sharing

The bot does not sell personal information or share collected information for advertising.

5. Data storage

Data is not intentionally retained beyond what is necessary for the bot's operation, except for any locally configured settings or logs.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot owner through Discord.
